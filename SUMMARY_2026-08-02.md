  - **Level 2 to 5 (Upline Sponsors)**: Every qualified upline sponsor up to 5 levels deep in the referral hierarchy receives their respective level commission (e.g. Level 2 = ৳250, Level 3 = ৳100, etc.).
- **Atomic Wallet Credits**: All qualified upline referrers receive their commission payouts directly into their wallets in real-time within a single atomic database transaction.
